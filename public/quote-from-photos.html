How it works

From the driveway to a sent quote

No more writing quotes at the kitchen table at night.

Snap

Take up to 6 photos of the job on your phone: the old unit, the problem, the space.

Say it

Tap the mic and say what the client needs, the way you’d tell your office. Or type it.

Check the draft

In about 30 seconds you get line items from your price list, options to choose from, and anything worth double-checking.

Send

Change what you like and send it. The client approves, signs and pays the deposit online.

01Your prices, not a guess

Built from your own price list

The draft uses the products and services you’ve already saved, with your names, descriptions and prices. The AI never makes up a price: if the job needs something that isn’t on your list, it’s added at $0 and highlighted, and the quote can’t go out until you price it.

  • ✓Your numbers, every timePrices come straight from your list, never from the AI.
  • ✓Nothing slips throughAnything without a price is flagged until you set one.
  • ✓Questions before you sendIf the photos don’t show something important, like the breaker size, the draft says so.
02Good / Better / Best

Options that win bigger jobs

When the job has real choices (repair, replace, or replace with the premium model), the draft lays them out side by side and marks the one you’d recommend. Seeing the choices side by side makes the bigger job an easy yes, and the client approves the one they want right on their phone.

  • ✓One option when that’s all it needsA simple job gets a simple quote.
  • ✓Shared items in every optionA service call or haul-away goes in each one automatically.
03Talk, don’t type

Built for gloves and ladders

Tap the mic and talk, the way you’d explain the job to your office. The photos and your notes are saved on the quote for your team, so the job starts with everything you saw on site.

  • ✓Works on any phoneTalk with the mic button, or your keyboard’s own microphone.
  • ✓Nothing is sent without youEvery draft waits for you to check it and press Send.

Questions

Snap to quote: FAQ

Does the AI set my prices?

No. Prices come from the products and services you’ve saved. Anything that isn’t on your list is added at $0 and highlighted, and the quote can’t be sent until you price it.

Is anything sent to my client automatically?

No. Snap to quote makes a draft. You check it, change anything you like, and send it yourself.

Which plan includes it?

Unlimited, with a monthly allowance of drafts that renews on the 1st. It’s in beta while we polish it.

Do I need a price list first?

It works best with one, because that’s where your names and prices come from. You can import your list from a spreadsheet in minutes. Without one, every item is added at $0 for you to price.

Can I talk instead of typing?

Yes. Tap the mic button and talk, or use the microphone on your phone’s keyboard.

What happens to the photos?

They’re saved on the quote as an internal note with what you said, so your team has them when the job starts. Clients don’t see internal notes.
