Questions

Good / Better / Best quotes: FAQ

Which plan includes it?

Advanced and Unlimited, and every free trial. It’s new, and we’re improving it with feedback from the businesses using it.

How many options can a quote have?

Two or three. A simple job can still have a single price, and you can turn options off any time without losing a line.

Does my client need an account or an app?

No. They open the quote from the link in your email or text, compare the options, and approve one on any phone or computer.

How do deposits work with options?

The discount, tax and deposit apply to whichever option the client picks. A percentage deposit is a percentage of that option’s total.

What if they tell me their choice on the phone?

Open the quote, mark it approved and choose the option they picked. Collecting their signature on your phone asks for the option too.

What goes on the job and the invoice?

Only the chosen option’s line items. The quote PDF lists every option with its totals, or just the chosen one after they decide.
