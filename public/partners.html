FAQ

Questions creators ask

Do I need a big following?

No. A few thousand followers who run trade businesses are worth more to us than a big general audience. Tell us about your channel and we’ll take a look.

What counts as my sign-up?

A business that signs up with your code, or within a couple of months of opening your link, even if it starts with the free trial first. You earn once it starts paying.

When and how do I get paid?

Each amount you earn is confirmed after a short hold, as long as the business is still paying. We pay confirmed amounts every month by PayPal, Venmo or bank transfer. Your stats page shows what’s on hold, what’s confirmed and what you’ve been paid.

Do I have to use Worklio Pro myself?

It helps a lot. Videos of real jobs work far better than reading a script, so we set approved creators up with an account to use on their own work.

Do I have to say it’s sponsored?

Yes. Whenever you mention us, use your network’s “Paid partnership” label or say #ad. The FTC requires it, and your followers will trust you more for it.

Can I run ads with my code?

Ads on your own channels are fine. Please don’t bid on our name in search ads or pretend to be us. Ask us first if you’re not sure.

Can I see how I’m doing?

Yes. You get a private stats page with your link visits, sign-ups, who’s paying and what you’ve earned, updated every time you open it.
