How it works

Marketing that happens while you work

No new habits. Your crew does what they already do, and the posts come out of it.

Questions

Every job markets itself: FAQ

Does anything get posted without me seeing it?

No. Each post waits in Marketing under Ready to post until someone who can post taps Post now. You can change the photos and caption, or skip it.

Which plans include it?

Advanced and Unlimited, along with social media posting to your connected accounts. It’s in beta while we polish it.

What if a photo shows a face or a house number?

Untick that photo before you post. Captions never name the client, the street or the house number, only the town, but you decide which photos go out.

Which social accounts can I post to?

Facebook, Instagram, Google Business Profile, LinkedIn, Threads, Pinterest, Bluesky and X. You connect your own business accounts once in Marketing.

Where do the photos come from?

From the job: photos in the Project Gallery (before, after and progress), photos in the job’s notes and photos in its checklists. Before and after photos go first.

How do you count the requests my posts bring in?

Each post links to your online booking page with a code for that post. When someone opens the page or sends a booking request from it, it’s counted for that post.

Can I turn it off?

Yes. The account owner or an admin can turn off posts from finished jobs in Marketing at any time, and you can still post by hand whenever you like.
