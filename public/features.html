01Quotes

Send polished quotes in minutes, not evenings

Pick services from your price list, adjust quantities, and the totals and sales tax add up for you. Send the quote by email or text while you’re still in the client’s driveway.

  • ✓Built from your price listSaved services fill in the name, description and price.
  • ✓Deposits up frontAsk for a flat or percentage deposit before the work starts.
  • ✓Always know where it standsDraft, awaiting response, approved or converted at a glance.
02Online approvals

Clients approve with one tap, no printer needed

Every quote has its own secure link. Your client reviews the line items on their phone, approves it, and pays the deposit on the spot. You get notified right away.

  • ✓E-signaturesThe client’s approval and signature are saved with the quote.
  • ✓Approved quote to jobTurn it into a scheduled job with every line item carried over.
03Scheduling

Every job, task and event on one calendar, color-coded by team member. Click an open time to book a job, and drag it to a new slot when plans change.

  • ✓Time off built inApproved time off shows up on the schedule automatically.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Are all 15 features in every plan?

The core tools for quotes, invoices and your price list are in every plan. Features like scheduling, online booking, card payments and reports depend on the plan you choose. The pricing page shows exactly what each plan includes.

Do my technicians need to install an app?

No. Everyone signs in from the web browser on their phone, tablet or computer. On a phone the app shows a simple bottom menu made for working in the field.

How do my clients pay?

Each invoice and quote has a secure pay link. Clients can pay by card through your own Stripe account, or by Zelle using your details. Payments and deposits are recorded on the invoice automatically.

Can I bring my existing client list?

Yes. Import clients from a CSV spreadsheet, then merge any duplicates in a couple of clicks.
