Questions

Frequently asked questions

Are all 23 features in every plan?

Quotes, invoices, your price list, recurring jobs, the client portal and client languages are in every plan. Advanced adds card payments, texting, online booking, Good / Better / Best quote options, reports, service plans, the dispatch map, time tracking, team location, job checklists & forms, job profit and QuickBooks & Xero sync. Unlimited adds Snap to quote, AI writing help, custom roles and your own domain, plus the Marketing suite: reviews, social posting, referral links, automations and weekly post suggestions. The Marketing suite is also an add-on for Core and Advanced. The pricing page shows exactly what each plan includes.

Do my technicians need to install an app?

No. Everyone signs in from the web browser on their phone, tablet or computer. On a phone the app shows a simple bottom menu made for working in the field.

How do my clients pay?

Each invoice and quote has a secure pay link. Clients can pay by card or bank transfer (ACH) through your own Stripe account, or with Zelle, Venmo, Cash App or PayPal using your details. You can let them pay part of an invoice now and the rest later. Payments and deposits are recorded on the invoice automatically.

Can my clients get their quotes in their own language?

Yes. Set a client’s language and their quotes, invoices, emails, PDFs, pay page and client portal appear in it. Your line items and notes stay as you typed them. Spanish is ready today, with more languages on the way. Need a different one? Request it from App Settings.

Can I bring my existing client list?

Yes. Import clients from a CSV spreadsheet, then merge any duplicates in a couple of clicks.
