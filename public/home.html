Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do my technicians need to install anything?

No. Everyone signs in from the web browser on their phone, tablet or computer. On a phone the app shows a simple bottom menu made for working in the field.

How do my clients pay?

Each invoice and quote has a secure pay link. Clients can pay by card through your own Stripe account, or by Zelle using your details. Payments and deposits are recorded on the invoice automatically.

Can I control what my team sees?

Yes. Owners and admins see everything, managers run jobs, quotes and invoices, and technicians see their schedule, clients and jobs without any prices.

Can I bring my existing client list?

Yes. Import clients from a CSV spreadsheet, then merge any duplicates in a couple of clicks.

Can I schedule jobs that repeat every week or month?

Yes, on every plan. Set a job to repeat daily, weekly on chosen days, or monthly, and every visit is scheduled ahead with its own team, time and invoice. You can change a single visit or every visit that follows.

Can I sell maintenance plans or memberships?

Yes. Service plans bill members every month, quarter or year, schedule the visits the plan includes, email each invoice with a pay link and give members their discount automatically. Service plans are on Advanced and Unlimited.

Does it track my team’s hours?

Yes. Team members clock in and out on the job from their phone. Managers approve timesheets, see overtime and export them for payroll, and hours count as labor cost in the Job profit report.

Can my crew fill in checklists on the job?

Yes. Build checklists and forms with checkboxes, Pass / Fail, notes, numbers and photos, and add them to a job, to every job, or to jobs with a certain service. Your team fills them in on their phone, a required one has to be finished before the job is marked complete, and you can share the finished checklist with your client or send it as a PDF. Checklists are on Advanced and Unlimited.

Does it work with QuickBooks or Xero?

Yes. Connect QuickBooks Online or Xero and clients, invoices, payments and expenses are sent over automatically every 15 minutes. The sync is in beta on plans with financial reports.

Is there a contract?

Plans are billed monthly or yearly. See the pricing page for the plans and what each one includes.
