Pricing questions

Do I pay extra for each team member?

Each plan includes the team logins shown on it. On Advanced, each login past the 5 included is $15 a month, about half what other field service apps charge per user. Unlimited has no limit on logins, for one price.

Will my price go up?

Not while you stay on your plan. If we raise a plan’s price, businesses already on it keep the price they signed up at, monthly or yearly.

What happens to a sale price when the sale ends?

You keep it. Subscribe while a plan is on sale, such as our Early Bird pricing, and that price is yours for as long as you stay on the plan, including if you switch between monthly and yearly billing.

How many text messages can I send?

Advanced includes 500 texts a month and Unlimited 2,000, for quotes, invoices, reminders, On My Way texts and automations. Core doesn’t include texting. Each text counts once, however long it is, and emails never count. We’ll let you know when 80% are used. If you reach the limit, texts start again on the 1st and emails keep going; you can move up a plan at any time for more right away.

Which plan has recurring jobs, service plans and the dispatch map?

Recurring jobs are in every plan. Service plans & memberships, the dispatch map with route optimization, time tracking & timesheets, job checklists & forms, job profit, QuickBooks & Xero sync and Every job markets itself (social posts from your finished jobs) are on Advanced and Unlimited. Automations, Snap to quote (a quote drafted from job photos), AI writing help, custom roles and your own domain are on Unlimited.

Are there fees on card payments?

Card payments go through your own Stripe account, so Stripe’s standard processing fees apply and payouts go straight to your bank. Zelle payments have no fee from us.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Move up or down from Account & Billing in the app as your business grows.

Is there a contract?

No long-term contract. Plans are billed monthly, or yearly at a lower price, and you can cancel anytime in Account & Billing.
